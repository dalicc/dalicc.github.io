History

Every version of the DALICC vocabulary and of the 8 dependency graphs, with the date it was published, its content hash and what changed. A content hash is computed from the canonical form dalicc-c14n-1, so two files with the same hash say the same thing, whatever the order of their statements. The hashes are the ones https://api.dalicc.net answers for the same versions.
